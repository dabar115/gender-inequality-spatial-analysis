# Raw data

Place the provincial panel here as **`provincial_indicators.csv`** (long format, one row per province and year):

| column | description | source |
|---|---|---|
| `province` | province name (Turkish spelling, e.g. `İstanbul`, `Afyonkarahisar`) | – |
| `year` | 2009 … 2018 | – |
| `EGT_KAD` | share of women aged 25+ with at least upper-secondary education (%) | TÜİK, National Education Statistics |
| `EGT_ESZ` | gap between female and male share aged 25+ with at least upper-secondary education | TÜİK |
| `ERG_DOG` | births to mothers under 19 per 1,000 births | TÜİK, Birth Statistics |
| `KIST` | women's share of compulsorily insured employees (%) | SGK, Statistical Yearbooks |
| `KBGDO` | GDP per capita in USD | TÜİK, Regional GDP |

81 provinces × 10 years = 810 rows. The raw data is not committed to the repository.
